## 🛡️ Overview

Warhammer 40,000 is not a game of luck. It is a game of probabilities wearing power armor. WH40K-Vox-Companion exists for the tacticians who want to see those probabilities laid bare — not to replace their judgment, but to sharpen it like a chainsword before the first dice roll.

This repository hosts the 2026 edition of a modular companion toolkit that sits alongside your Warhammer 40,000 sessions. It observes, calculates, and recommends. You decide. The Emperor protects, but preparation protects better.

Whether you are running a casual 500-point skirmish in your local hobby shop or orchestrating a 3,000-point narrative campaign across a weekend, this companion adapts to your scale, your faction, and your appetite for detail.

## 🌌 Why This Exists

Most tactical aids either overwhelm you with raw statistics or hide their logic behind opaque formulas. WH40K-Vox-Companion takes a third path: it explains *why* a recommendation exists, shows the assumptions behind every calculation, and lets you override anything with a single command.

Think of it as a seasoned veteran whispering in your ear — one who has read every codex, memorized every stratagem, and never once complains about the noise.

## 🏗️ Architecture at a Glance

The companion is organized into four conceptual layers, each removable and replaceable:

1. **Observation Layer** — Reads game state inputs from manual entry, voice, or imported roster files.
2. **Reasoning Layer** — Applies faction rules, terrain modifiers, and scenario constraints to produce candidate actions.
3. **Presentation Layer** — Renders insight through charts, annotations, and plain-language explanations.
4. **Persistence Layer** — Stores profiles, replays, and preferences in portable, human-readable formats.

This separation means you can swap out a single layer without disturbing the rest — a principle borrowed from modular fortress design.

## 🧪 Recommended Workflow

- **Before a match:** Build or import your roster, then run a quick scenario simulation to identify weak links.
- **During a match:** Keep the companion in compact mode for glanceable recommendations and quick probability checks.
- **After a match:** Open the session replay, mark turning points, and export a battle report for future reference.

Over time, this loop turns every game into a lesson, and every lesson into an edge.
